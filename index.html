Project Synopsis

A legend of two sisters, brought back to life

Set in the scenic landscapes of Sarawak, Santubong: Legenda Yang Hidup follows the legendary sisters Puteri Santubong, admired for her graceful weaving, and Puteri Sejinjang, known for her strength in cultivating rice. Though they once lived in harmony, growing jealousy and pride turned their bond into a bitter rivalry that ended in tragic conflict.

Through cinematic storytelling, striking visuals and elements of Sarawak's cultural heritage, the project explores sisterhood, jealousy and the consequences of anger, while preserving local folklore through digital innovation.

Places of the Legend

Two princesses who became the landscape of Sarawak

Bukit Santubong

Puteri Santubong, the weaver

Mount Santubong rises beside the Sarawak coast. In the legend, Puteri Santubong was transformed into this mountain after the quarrel. Its jagged peak is said to be the scar from her sister's blow, and from a distance the mountain looks like a woman lying down.

Pulau Sejinjang

Puteri Sejinjang, the rice cultivator

Struck by her sister's weaving tool, Puteri Sejinjang was cursed to become part of the landscape too. In many tellings she became Mount Sejinjang, and the pieces of her shattered form scattered into the sea as the islands near Santubong, such as Pulau Kera, Pulau Satang and Pulau Talang-Talang.
